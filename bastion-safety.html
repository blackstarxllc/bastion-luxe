Safety Center

Bastion's protections go a long way — but a little awareness goes further.

Escrow protects the money. It's still worth knowing what scammers actually try, so nothing catches you off guard.

Golden rule: never release funds until you've personally confirmed the item — in person or in hand — matches the listing exactly.

For buyers

Never pay outside of Bastion/Escrow.com

If a seller asks you to pay them directly, via a separate app, or "to save on fees" — that's the single most common trick to get you outside of any protection. Decline every time.

Inspect before you release

Whether meeting in person or receiving a shipment, check the item matches the listing (condition, authenticity, serial numbers) before confirming receipt. Once released, funds can't be pulled back.

Be wary of urgency or pressure

"I need this released right now" or "I have another buyer waiting" are pressure tactics. A legitimate seller will never rush you past a genuine inspection.

Check seller history

Look for a Trusted Seller badge, completed trade count, and real reviews on their profile before buying from someone new.

For sellers

Wait for real confirmation before handing off the item

A buyer claiming "I already sent the wire" means nothing until Bastion shows the transaction as genuinely funded. Never ship or hand off an item before that.

Use tracked shipping

Always ship with tracking to the address Escrow.com provides. It protects you if there's ever a dispute about delivery.

Don't share your release code early

For in-person trades, your release code should only ever be given to the buyer once they're standing in front of you and you're both satisfied with the trade — never before.

For in-person meetups

Meet in public, well-lit places

Bank lobbies and designated police department "safe exchange zones" are ideal — both are available as one-tap suggestions on the transaction page.

Bring a friend if the item is high-value

There's no downside to having a second person present for a large transaction.

If something feels wrong

Use "Report a problem with this trade" on the transaction page immediately — this freezes the funds in escrow and brings in a real review, instead of the situation being only your word against theirs.
